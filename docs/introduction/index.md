# Introduction

This is a practical LangChain tutorial written from a beginner's perspective. As I learn and build, I will explain the questions I encounter and turn working examples into a series of connected chapters.

## Who is this for?

Developers who want to start building AI applications and prefer learning through examples. The tutorial will introduce concepts where they become useful, then connect them to practical steps.

## How to read

Follow the [contents](../catalogue/) for the suggested reading order, or jump to a topic you need. Each article focuses on a clear subject and can also be read on its own.

The tutorial will grow one article at a time. Planned topics are listed separately in the contents; they are not completed lessons. Currently, only the introduction, reading plan, and preparation page are available. Runnable LangChain examples have not been added yet.

## Articles and example code

Explanations and selected code snippets belong in `hello-langchain-doc`. Complete runnable examples are planned for the separate `hello-langchain` project. Chapters will link to their corresponding examples as they become available.

English and Simplified Chinese pages share matching paths. Use the language menu to switch versions of the current chapter.
